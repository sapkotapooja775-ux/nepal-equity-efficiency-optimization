# Equity-Efficiency Trade-offs in Public Investment Allocation in Nepal-

## Overview

This project develops a multi-objective Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) framework to investigate the trade-off between economic efficiency and regional equity in public investment allocation across Nepal's seven provinces.

The model incorporates regional economic productivity, infrastructure development gaps, poverty, population, and investment requirements to identify alternative public investment allocation strategies.

## Research Objectives

- Measure regional economic productivity and infrastructure development gaps.
- Construct provincial investment-need indicators.
- Maximise expected economic benefits from public investment.
- Minimise regional disparities in investment allocation.
- Apply the epsilon-constraint method to generate alternative efficient solutions.
- Examine the trade-off between economic efficiency and regional equity.
- Evaluate alternative government budget and policy scenarios.

## Methodology

**Stage 1: Regional Indicator Construction**

Provincial economic, infrastructure, poverty, and population indicators are standardised and combined to estimate regional investment needs.

**Stage 2: Multi-Objective Optimisation**

A multi-objective MILP model is developed with economic efficiency and regional equity as competing objectives.

**Stage 3: Epsilon-Constraint Method**

The model maximises economic benefits subject to alternative equity constraints to generate a Pareto trade-off frontier.

**Stage 4: Scenario and Sensitivity Analysis**

Alternative budget levels, regional need weights, and investment assumptions are examined.

## Study Area

Nepal's seven provinces:

- Koshi
- Madhesh
- Bagmati
- Gandaki
- Lumbini
- Karnali
- Sudurpashchim
